# TIC 50717149: check of a possible single transit in Sector 99 (PHT Talk 2110/4162521)

Reported on Planet Hunters TESS: a possible ~0.4 d transit at "day ~23.01" of the Sector 99 subject.

- The PHT day axis starts at the sector's first timestamp (BTJD 4046.114), so day 23.01 is BTJD ~4069.12.
- `event_s99.png`: SPOC 2-min Sector 99 light curve around that time (PDCSAP, SAP, background, centroids; black = 15-cadence
  running mean). A 0.4 d box at BTJD 4069.12 gives 87 ± 108 ppm; a 5 R⊕ planet on this star would make ~1,700–2,700 ppm.
  0.4 d boxes across the whole sector scatter by ~340 ppm.
- A blind KESTREL search of Sectors 7, 34, 61, 88 and 99 (development version) found no periodic transit signal.
- Gaia DR3 3064786618300390272: Teff 6343 K, R 1.24 R☉ (FLAME), [M/H] −0.7 (GSP-Spec), RUWE 4.7.
